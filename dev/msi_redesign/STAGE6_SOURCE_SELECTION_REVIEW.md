# Stage 6 — Source Selection Review Specification (design only; development / RUO)

**This document defines HOW a future validation source will be selected. It selects no source, names no source as preferred or excluded, and does not change the governance gate,
which remains `STAGE 6 GOVERNANCE GATE: NOT CLOSED — EXTERNAL RESOLUTION REQUIRED`.** No patient-level or controlled-access data was accessed; no access requested; no provider contacted; no DAC request submitted; no cohort constructed;
no patient identifier inspected; no classifier trained or tuned; no MSI threshold or production code touched. Rules are generic and contain no numeric thresholds, minimum sample counts or partition sizes; those belong to the Stage 6B Analysis Plan, which is not created here.
Note: the instruction that requested this document was cut off in section 5; sections 1–4 are specified as given, section 5 is completed from its stated headings, and section 6 is added by me so the specification is usable (marked below).
Date: 2026-09-25.

## 1. Purpose
Source Selection Review (SSR) decides, by predefined criteria and recorded evidence, whether a candidate data source is **eligible for a stated role** in Stage 6 MSI empirical validation. Binding statements:
* SSR occurs **only after the applicable governance conditions are resolved** (`STAGE6_GOVERNANCE_GATE_STATUS.md`). Until then SSR may not begin.
* **No source is selected by this document.**
* The eventual validation dataset must be **independent from development and tuning data**.
* **GenRichi output must never be treated as ground truth**, in any role and at any stage.
* Cell-line or other control material is never a substitute for independent patient-level validation.
* Eligibility is **role-specific**; a source eligible for one role is not thereby eligible for another. Four roles are kept separate:
  * **Development data** — exploratory work, locus evidence, parameter exploration; never the locked validation set.
  * **Locked validation data** — the frozen, independent set used once under §4; must satisfy every requirement in §2 and no condition in §3.
  * **Supporting/reference evidence** — corroborating labels or annotations (tiers P3–P5 in §2A and computational evidence) used to interpret or cross-check; never ground truth and never validation labels.
  * **Cell-line/analytical controls** — development or control material only; never a substitute for independent patient-level validation.
* **Availability or accessibility never confers a role.** A source does not become the validation source because it is available, public, large or easy to access; validation eligibility follows only from the §2 requirements being documented and no §3 condition applying.

## 2. Required dataset characteristics (mandatory for a validation candidate)

### A. Label provenance
The candidate must have an **independently established MSI status**: established by a party and method independent of GenRichi and of the development work, with documented method, laboratory/tool, definition of the MSI-H/MSS (and any intermediate) classes, and date.
Evidence is ordered by provenance quality as **ordinal tiers, not scores**; no weighting or numerical scoring is applied:
| Tier | Evidence type | Handling |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | Validated clinical/reference assay on the specimen (e.g. accepted PCR/fragment-analysis panel with documented markers and class definition) | Eligible as reference label when provenance is documented per specimen |
| P2 | Orthogonal laboratory method with documented performance | Eligible as reference label when documented; concordance with P1 recorded where both exist |
| P3 | Authoritative pathology/MMR evidence where appropriate (e.g. MMR immunohistochemistry) | Corroborating; measures mismatch-repair status, not the same construct as microsatellite instability, so concordance is recorded, not assumed |
| P4 | Well-documented computational consensus (multiple independent tools with a documented rule) | **Supporting evidence only** under the standing owner ruling that computational labels are not ground truth; changing that ruling requires a recorded owner decision |
| P5 | Publication-derived labels (label copied from a paper or table without access to the underlying assay record) | Supporting evidence only; original assay provenance must be traced before any elevation |
| — | **Labels generated by GenRichi (any stage, any parameter set), whether directly or indirectly** — including labels that incorporate, are adjusted, filtered or adjudicated by GenRichi output, specimens included or excluded on the basis of GenRichi calls, and consensus rules in which GenRichi is a member | **NOT ACCEPTABLE as ground truth** — never eligible |
Further rules: all available evidence per specimen is recorded, not just the best; discordant evidence is kept and adjudicated by a **rule declared before results are inspected**; labels are never re-assigned after the validation set is locked;
intermediate/indeterminate classes (for example MSI-low) are carried as their own states, not silently merged. No current source is declared here to satisfy these requirements.

### B. Patient/sample independence
The candidate must provide:
* **patient-level independence from development data** (no patient contributes to both);
* no duplicate specimens counted as independent observations;
* replicates, re-processings, multiple pieces, multiple sequencing runs and multi-centre re-sequencing of the same tumour handled **at patient/sample level**;
* documented provenance of the cohort (collection protocol, consent basis, origin of specimens);
* **overlap with every other candidate dataset identified before validation**, using documented identifiers or authoritative statements; overlap that cannot be assessed is recorded as `NOT_ASSESSED`, never as "independent".
Independence is never inferred from different accession numbers, papers, repositories or institutions. An overlap or independence status recorded as `NOT_ASSESSED` can change **only** by documented authoritative evidence entered in the review record (§6); it is never converted to "independent" by assumption, default, elapsed time, or the absence of an overlap statement.

### C. Assay and specimen metadata
Minimum metadata, recorded per specimen **where available**; anything missing is recorded explicitly as `NOT_ASSESSED` and is **never silently inferred, imputed or defaulted**:
specimen type · tumour/normal status (and matched-normal identity) · assay type (exome, genome, panel; capture design) · MSI assay method · label source · tumour purity/tumour content (with the estimation method) · preservation (FFPE/fresh/frozen; other pre-analytical variables such as ischaemia time where documented) · sequencing platform and read length · coverage/quality metrics relevant to repeat-length measurement · reference genome build and contig naming · processing pipeline and version.
Insufficient metadata to interpret the reference label is an exclusion/pause condition (§3), not a gap to fill by assumption.

### D. Access and governance
The candidate must have: a **lawful access route**; **applicable current terms documented** from an authoritative source; an **intended use compatible with the authorization** (no assumption from silence, from "commercial products" language, or from the existence of a public file); documented provenance; a defined **storage and processing location**; identified **data-protection requirements** (security, retention/destruction, redistribution limits, reporting); and confirmation that requester eligibility is established.

## 3. Candidate exclusion criteria (generic; no dataset is named)
Two outcomes, recorded with the evidence: **PAUSED** — a condition is *unresolved* and may be resolved later, after which the candidate returns to review; **DISQUALIFIED FOR THE ROLE** — a documented adverse fact makes the candidate unsuitable for that role. Permission is never assumed from commercial-product language, from silence in an agreement, or from public availability of files.
| Condition | Outcome |
|---|---|
| Unclear or unresolved label provenance | **PAUSED** |
| Unresolved data-use terms | **PAUSED** |
| Unresolved access route (no lawful route documented) | **PAUSED** |
| Unresolved commercial/diagnostic-use restrictions | **PAUSED** |
| Unresolved cohort overlap with other candidates | **PAUSED** |
| Unknown accession-to-cohort mapping (which patients the cohort is) | **PAUSED** |
| Patient/sample independence not yet established for a role that requires it | **PAUSED** |
| Duplicate/replicate structure not yet resolved to patient/sample level | **PAUSED** |
| Insufficient metadata to interpret the reference label | **PAUSED** |
| Data-protection, storage or processing requirements not identified | **PAUSED** |
| Intended use documented as incompatible with the applicable authorization or terms | **DISQUALIFIED FOR THE ROLE** |
| Labels derived from GenRichi, directly or indirectly (§2A) | **DISQUALIFIED** (as reference labels) |
| Data used for development, locus selection, parameter setting or tuning, considered for the locked validation role | **DISQUALIFIED FOR THE VALIDATION ROLE** |
| Overlap with development data documented and not separable at patient level, or independence documented as unattainable for the role | **DISQUALIFIED FOR THAT ROLE** (other roles are assessed separately) |
| Duplicate/replicate structure documented as impossible to assign to patient/sample level | **DISQUALIFIED FOR THE ROLE** |

## 4. Development vs locked validation
Three conceptual states apply to every dataset (a dataset is in exactly one state for a given analysis):
* **`DEVELOPMENT`** — exploratory work is allowed: characterization, locus evidence, parameter exploration, method checks.
* **`LOCKED VALIDATION`** — membership, labels, exclusions, analysis plan and parameters are frozen and hash-recorded; the data are not used to choose thresholds, loci or classifier changes.
* **`POST-VALIDATION`** — results are reported as obtained; no re-tuning against the same data.
Rules:
1. Development data may be used for exploratory work only; nothing learned from locked validation data may influence threshold selection or classifier modification.
2. **Pre-registration:** the analysis plan is pre-registered — dated, immutable and hash-recorded — **before** the locked validation set is opened. Before locking, the following are frozen and hashed: the source-review decision record, the membership rule and list (by patient key), the label table and its provenance, exclusion rules, the pre-declared adjudication rule, all parameters/thresholds (fixed from development data only), and the analysis plan.
3. **Opening the locked validation set is a controlled event**: authorized in writing by a named owner, logged (who, when, purpose, hashes of everything opened, environment), single-purpose, and limited to executing the frozen plan.
4. **Any post-lock change** to labels, membership, exclusions, thresholds, or the analysis plan is recorded as a **deviation** (date, reason, whether any outcome had been seen, who approved).
5. If the validation set is **materially changed after results are inspected**, or a deviation was made after seeing outcomes, the run **cannot be presented as the original locked validation**; the affected data become development data and a new independent set is required.
6. Re-tuning after inspecting validation results moves the data to `DEVELOPMENT` permanently; a new independent locked set is then required.
7. Data already analyzed during earlier Stage 6 work (for example Stage 6A) are `DEVELOPMENT` data and cannot later be presented as locked validation.
8. No public label is treated as ground truth merely because it is public or published.

## 5. Partitioning rules (rules only; no partition is constructed and no sizes or counts are defined)
* **Patient-level partitioning:** the unit of assignment is the patient (with a stable, documented patient key). Every specimen, aliquot, replicate, re-sequencing, re-alignment, multiple tumour piece, tumour/normal pair and multi-centre duplicate of a patient goes to the **same** partition.
* **No leakage through replicates:** technical or processing replicates never count as independent observations; a matched normal stays with its own patient's partition (it is never shared across patients or partitions), and a tumour and its own normal are not split.
* **No patient appearing in more than one partition**, and none appearing in more than one source with different roles; a patient present in several candidate sources is one patient, assigned once.
* **Cross-source overlap is resolved before assignment**; where overlap cannot be assessed the sources are treated as potentially overlapping and are not given opposite (development vs validation) roles on the assumption of independence.
* **Assignment is deterministic and recorded before any label-dependent analysis**, by a predeclared rule based only on the stable patient key, not on label, coverage, outcome or any Stage 3 ranking attribute.
* **No leakage through derived choices:** loci, thresholds, cutoffs, missing-data rules and adjudication rules are fixed from development data only; validation patients are not used to select candidate loci or to set any parameter.
* Relatedness between patients, batch/site structure and pre-analytical variables are recorded where documented; where unknown they are `NOT_ASSESSED`.
* Numbers of patients, class balance and minimum evidence are **not defined here** and are decided by the owner in the Stage 6B Analysis Plan.

## 6. Review procedure (added to make this specification operational)
1. Preconditions: governance gate closed for the candidate's applicable items, documented and hash-recorded.
2. Per-candidate evidence record covering §2A–D, one field per requirement, with `NOT_ASSESSED` where not established, each entry citing an authoritative source and its evidence quality.
3. Apply §3; record each outcome (DISQUALIFIED FOR THE ROLE / PAUSED / not triggered).
4. Role-specific outcome per candidate: `ELIGIBLE FOR ROLE`, `PAUSED`, `DISQUALIFIED FOR ROLE`, or `NOT_ASSESSED`; no ranking score and no automatic "best".
5. Second review: an **independent review of the evidence and of the decision record**; disagreements recorded, not overwritten.
6. Owner decision: a **governance decision**, recorded with its evidence IDs and traceable to the evidence (no decision without cited evidence).
   **Neither the second review nor the owner decision constitutes approval of a source unless all applicable governance gates are already closed.**
7. Any later change to the record follows §4 deviation rules once a lock has occurred.

It does not select or exclude any named source, does not state that any dataset is authorized, does not define the Stage 6B Analysis Plan, thresholds, minimum sample counts or partitions, does not modify code, and is not legal advice.
