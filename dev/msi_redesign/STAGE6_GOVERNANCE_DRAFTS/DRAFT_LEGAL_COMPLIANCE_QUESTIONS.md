## Questions for qualified reviewers
1. **Compatibility with every applicable agreement.** Is the intended use described above compatible with each applicable agreement — the dbGaP Data Use Certification Agreement (Ver. January 25, 2025) and addenda for `phs000178`, `phs001287` and, if relevant, `phs000892`; the CPTAC-specific term referred to in the `phs001287` addendum (text not yet available); the Sanger Cancer Dependency Map Data Usage Policy; the EGA/DAC terms for EGAD00001001039; the CC BY 4.0 licence on DepMap Public releases and the DepMap/CCLE portal terms (unread)?
2. **Diagnostic-product development and "approved research purpose".** Does development of a diagnostic product, as the downstream purpose of methodological research, fall within an "approved research project" under Term 1 ("solely in connection with the approved research project described in the DAR") and within any applicable data-use limitation? What would a truthful Research Use Statement need to say?
3. **Commercial-product language.** How should Term 9 be interpreted in this context — that "there is no restriction on development of commercial products resulting from the knowledge gained from the research project", read together with NIH's statements that the data are "pre-competitive", that Approved Users are urged "to avoid making IP claims derived directly from the dataset(s)", and that data and conclusions "will remain freely available, without requirement for licensing"? Does it bear on a proprietary analysis approach or panel?
4. **Derived evidence tables and genomic summary results.** What is the status of locus-level, aggregate evidence tables derived from controlled-access data? Are they "Data Derivatives" (which "may not be sold … for any purpose") or "genomic summary results" (public posting "Not Allowed" in the TCGA addendum and "Allowed" in the CPTAC addenda), and what follows for internal use, storage, sharing with collaborators, publication and inclusion in regulatory documentation?
5. **Requester eligibility.** Can a commercial/for-profit organization act as Requester, through what institutional roles (Institutional Signing Official, eRA Commons registration, a PI meeting the agreement's definition), and does the agreements' silence on for-profit status require confirmation from the provider before proceeding?
6. **Sanger product incorporation.** What restrictions apply under the Sanger policy to incorporation of results into a product, to "commercial services", and to the "internal proprietary research" boundary; and what consent is required, from whom?
7. **Third-party rights.** Which third-party rights must be examined — including the rights that CC BY 4.0 does not cover, cell-bank and CCLE contributor rights, and any rights asserted by submitters of public sequence records — before any use beyond internal research?
8. **Data protection after authorization.** Which data-protection requirements would begin upon authorization (for example the security best practices, incident reporting, retention and destruction at close-out, redistribution limits and one-year renewable access stated in the agreements), which storage and processing location satisfies them, and who at GenRichi is accountable?
9. **Limits on development use before clinical validation.** Are there any further restrictions, under the agreements, applicable law or regulation, on using the data in development work that precedes any clinical validation — including any expectation that development results not be represented as clinical performance?
