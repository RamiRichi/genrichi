**Subject:** Enquiry about cohort provenance and possible patient overlap between CPTAC prospective cohorts and TCGA

Dear [Recipient],

We are seeking clarification of the provenance of two CPTAC cohorts and whether any patients are shared with TCGA. **We do not assume that a "prospectively collected" cohort is independent of TCGA, and we do not treat the absence of an overlap statement in the literature as evidence that no overlap exists.** This enquiry is not a data-access request.

**What we have found in public documentation (for context).**
* A published descriptor states that the CPTAC retrospective colorectal proteomics study analysed 95 specimens from 90 TCGA patients, cut sequentially from the tumour blocks used for TCGA genomics. We regard this as documented overlap for that retrospective set only.
* The CPTAC colon paper (Vasaikar et al., Cell 2019) describes a prospectively collected cohort (110 patients; whole-exome sequencing of 106 tumours; collected "in strict accordance to the CPTAC-2 colon procurement protocol"). Open GDC metadata for project CPTAC-2 lists 105 colon-site cases and 1 rectum-site case; the dbGaP study `phs000892` describes 348 consented subjects across four tumour types.
* The CPTAC endometrial paper (Dou et al., Cell 2020) describes 95 prospectively collected tumours. Open GDC metadata for CPTAC-3 lists 241 cases with primary site "Uterus, NOS".
* None of the documents we read states whether any of these prospective patients is also a TCGA participant, and we could not establish the relationship between the numbers above.

## Questions
1. **Colon cohort provenance.** How was the prospective CPTAC colon cohort assembled (programme, enrolment, tissue source sites, consent), and is it distinct from any TCGA collection?
2. **Endometrial cohort provenance.** The same question for the CPTAC endometrial cohort.
3. **Cohort-to-accession mapping.** Is there an authoritative mapping of each cohort to accession(s) and case sets — for the colon cohort (110 patients / 106 exomes / GDC CPTAC-2 colon-site cases / `phs000892` / SRA `PRJNA514017`), and for the endometrial cohort (95 tumours versus 241 GDC "Uterus, NOS" cases)? Where is it published?
4. **Relationship to TCGA.** Is any patient, specimen or aliquot in either cohort also present in TCGA (including as a different specimen from the same patient)? Which programme records would show this?
5. **Was overlap assessed?** Was patient-level overlap with TCGA assessed when these cohorts were assembled or released, and if so by whom, how (for example identifier crosswalk or genotype concordance), and is the result documented?
6. **What evidence can establish independence?** What authoritative evidence would you regard as sufficient to establish, or refute, patient-level independence from TCGA (for example a provenance statement, a case-level crosswalk, or a check that an approved user could run under authorized access)?
7. **Case-level mapping.** Would establishing independence require a specific accession-and-case-level mapping, and is such a mapping available to us, or only to approved users?
8. **Other CPTAC cohorts.** Do the same considerations apply to other CPTAC cohorts that may be relevant (open GDC metadata lists CPTAC-3 gastric cases), and where is their provenance documented?

Please indicate if any answer can only be given to an authorized requester and what would be needed to obtain it. Thank you. [Name / role / contact details]
