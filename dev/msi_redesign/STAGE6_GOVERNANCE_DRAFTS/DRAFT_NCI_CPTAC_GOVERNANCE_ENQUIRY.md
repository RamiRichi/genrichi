## A. TCGA — dbGaP `phs000178`
1. **Applicable agreement.** We read the dbGaP Data Use Certification Agreement dated "Ver. January 25, 2025" with the study-specific addendum for `phs000178.v11.p8`. Could you confirm whether this is the current applicable agreement and version?

3. **Diagnostic-product development.** Term 9 states, among other things, that "there is no restriction on development of commercial products resulting from the knowledge gained from the research project" and that NIH "urges Approved Users to avoid making IP claims derived directly from the dataset(s)". Neither the agreement nor the addendum, as we read them, addresses diagnostic or clinical development. Could you confirm whether, and where, NIH or the DAC addresses use of controlled-access data in research whose downstream purpose includes a diagnostic product, and what determination or approval would be required?
4. **Commercial / for-profit context.** Could you confirm whether a commercial or for-profit organization may act as a Requester, and whether any additional conditions apply?
